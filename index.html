VNA Design — 0.5 to 10 GHz
6-layer 2-port VNA targeting >130 dB dynamic range. STM32H7, high-speed dual ADC, 100 Mbps Ethernet, USB-C. Full signal chain in Keysight ADS.
→
S-parameter sim — add image
6-layer stackup — add image
  • 6-layer 2-port VNA targeting >130 dB dynamic range across 0.5–10 GHz
  • STM32H7 digitizes downconverter outputs via dual ADC; 100 Mbps Ethernet for S-param transfer, USB-C Full Speed, SWD debug
  • 2.3–2.8 GHz Colpitts VCO: −1.8 to −11 dBm into 50 Ω, phase noise −169 dBc/Hz at 1 MHz offset
  • 90° hybrid coupler at 2.45 GHz: −3 dB coupling, −44 dB isolation; 180° rat-race: −3 dB over 2 GHz BW
  • Balanced mixer: −5.6 dB conversion loss; amplifier: S21 = 15.47 dB, S11 = −34.3 dB
  • Gap-coupled BPF: 6% fractional BW, S11 = −25.7 dB, S21 = −5.33 dB in passband
